Understand the reporting

Counts versus percentages

Enrollment counts students. Demographics show source percentages. MAP percentages describe tested students in a subject and student group, not all enrolled students. Overlapping groups are never added together.

Missing does not mean zero

Unavailable and suppressed values are omitted from graphs, not estimated. Each report states its coverage. Historical gaps remain gaps; separate race/ethnicity categories are not a complete composition.

Source periods stay separate

Historical enrollment ends in 2025. Preliminary 2025–26 enrollment is a separate view. Original enrollment and demographic extracts listed in the master’s register were not supplied; those values are traced to the supplied master.
