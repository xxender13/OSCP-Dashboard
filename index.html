Understand the reporting

Counts versus percentages

Enrollment counts students. FRL percentages use January membership. MAP proficiency describes reportable tested students. APR growth reports the share of eligible buildings designated Target, not the percentage of students growing.

Missing does not mean zero

Unavailable* values remain missing. A source * is suppression with no specified cause in these extracts; blank or NULL values are not reported. State demographic summaries include only paired reportable district counts and their denominators.

Source periods stay separate

Historical data ends in 2025. Enrollment is 2025–26 preliminary; demographics, MAP and APR are 2025. The building directory is undated. State MAP totals are all-grade context; no matched-grade above-state classification is available.
